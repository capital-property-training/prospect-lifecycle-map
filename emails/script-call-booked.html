Drafted 7 Oct 2026 from Hormozi’s speed-to-lead rules: name what they just did, confirm the time with a real person, get anyone else who decides on the call, give them a reason to turn up. No pitch, no questions about their situation. Under a minute. Highlighted parts change per person.
[First name]? (pause) It’s Ross, Ross Cheung.
I just saw your Setup Call come through for [day] at [time]. I wanted to ring and lock it in with you myself.
Will anyone else be part of the decision with you? A partner, business partner? (wait)
If yes: “Get them on the call with you, it’ll save going over it twice.” If they can’t make that time, move it now to one where both can.
And what town are you looking at for your first deal? (wait)
Perfect. I’ll pull the rent numbers for [town] before we speak, so we can go through them together.
If they booked 2+ days out and you have a gap sooner: “I’ve actually got a gap [today at / tomorrow at time] if you’d rather get going sooner.”
Best on a laptop if you can, I’ll be sharing my screen. See you [day] at [time].

No answer

Don’t say

  • “Thanks for booking”
  • “Is this still a good time for you?”
  • Anything about price or the programme
  • “Free call”. It’s the Setup Call that comes with their course
